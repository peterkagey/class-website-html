  1. [1] Prove or disprove that if \(N \triangleleft G\) and \(N \leq H \leq G\), then \(N \triangleleft H\).
  2. [2] Prove that there exists a homomorphism \(\phi\colon D_{12} \to S_4\) with \(\phi(r) = (1423)\) and \(\phi(f) = (13)(24)\), and prove that it is not an isomorphism by showing that it is not injective (1-to-1).
  3. [2] Give an example of two groups \(G\) and \(H\) and a group homomorphism \(\phi \colon G \to H\) such that \(G\) is non-abelian, \(H\) is abelian, and \(\phi\) is not the trivial homomorphism.
  4. [2] Give an example of a group \(G\) and a (normal) subgroup \(H \triangleleft G\) such that \(G/H\) is not abelian.
  5. [2] Let \(\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{R})\) denote the group of invertible \(n \times n\) real-valued matrices under matrix multiplication. Recall that a square matrix is invertible if and only if its determinant is non-zero and that \(\det(AB) = \det(A)\det(B)\). Let \[ \mathrm{SL}_n(\mathbb{R}) = \{A \in \mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{R}) \mid \det(A) = 1\}. \] Prove that \(\mathrm{SL}_n(\mathbb{R})\) is a normal subgroup of \(\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb R)\).
  6. [1] Explain why the following are true for every subgroup $H \leq G$:
    1. For all $g \in G$ and $h \in H$, $g * h * g^{-1} \in G$
    2. For all $g \in H$ and $h \in H$, $g * h * g^{-1} \in H$
  7. [2] Prove that for every group \(G\), \(G \times \mathbf{1}\) is isomorphic to \(G\) by constructing an explicit isomorphism. (Note that \(\mathbf{1} = \{e\} \) is the trivial group, which consists of only the identity.)
  8. [2] Prove that if $N_1 \triangleleft G_1$ and $N_2 \triangleleft G_2$, then $N_1 \times N_2 \triangleleft G_1 \times G_2$.
  9. [2] Prove or disprove the following: If \(G/N \cong H\) then \(G \cong N \times H\).
