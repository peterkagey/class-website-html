In this part, you will draw my favorite Cayley diagram.
  1. In the Cayley diagram for $S_4$ below, four of the vertices have been specified: $e$, $(12)$, $(23)$, and $(34)$. Use this to determine what the three different edges ("arrows") correspond to.
  2. This Cayley graph (i.e. Cayley diagram) is called a "polytope" because the vertex/edge configuration can be mapped onto the vertices and edges of a polyhedron. Go look up the list of the $13$ Archimedean solids, and determine which solid it corresponds to and state why.1
