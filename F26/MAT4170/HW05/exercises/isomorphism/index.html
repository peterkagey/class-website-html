  1. [4] Suppose that \(G = D_{12}\) and \(N = \langle r^3 \rangle\).
    1. Prove that \([G:N] = 6\) and \(N \triangleleft G\) is a normal subgroup.
    2. Make a Cayley diagram or Cayley table for the quotient group \(G/N\), and prove that it is isomorphic to \(S_3\) by giving an explicit isomorphism \({\phi \colon G/N \to S_3}\). (i.e., specify where each coset of \(N\) goes under \(\phi\).)
  2. [2] Prove that if \(G\) is a group, and \(H \leq G\) such that \([G:H] = 2\), then \(H \triangleleft G\) is a normal subgroup, and \(G/H\) is isomorphic to \(C_2\).
