  1. [1] Physically construct a tetrahedron, and label its corners \(1\), \(2\), \(3\), \(4\). Take a photo of it, and include it here using \includegraphics[...]{...}. We denote the rotational symmetry group of the tetrahedron by $T$.
  2. [1] Describe each of the \(12\) elements of $T$ using cycle notation.
  3. [1] Is there a symmetry in $T$ that sends the vertex \(1 \mapsto 2\), \(2 \mapsto 3\), \(3\mapsto 4\) and \(4\mapsto 1\)? Why or why not?
  4. [2] Write down the elements of the four conjugacy classes of $T$, choose a representative of each conjugacy class to describe in words, and prove algebraically that elements within a conjugacy class are indeed conjugate.
    1. \(c_1 = \{e, \dots\}\)
    2. \(c_2 = \{(123), \dots\}\)
    3. \(c_3 = \{(132), \dots\}\)
    4. \(c_4 = \{(12)(34), \dots\}\)
  5. [1] How does $T$ relate to the family of groups (alternating groups) that you saw in Exam 1, which were defined as elements of \(S_n\) that can be written as an even number of \(2\)-cycles?
  6. [1] Find a subgroup (besides the trivial group and the whole group) and show that it is not a normal subgroup of $T$ by first writing down all of the elements in the subgroup \(H \leq T\) and then describing explicit elements \(t \in T\) and \(h \in H\) such that \(t * h * t^{-1} \not\in H\).
  7. [1] Draw a Cayley diagram for $T$. Which generators did you use for the Cayley diagram?
  8. [1] Is \(T\) a normal subgroup of \(S_4\)? If so, what is \(S_4/T\) isomorphic to? If not, why not?
