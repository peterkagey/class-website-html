Okay, well these points aren't exactly free, but they're close!

Find some homework exercises that took you significantly longer than 10 minutes per point. For two points per exercise, write three sentences about the exercise that will help me and future students. (Below are some prompts that may help you figure out what to write.)

  1. Is there an idea, theorem, definition, or short hint that would have helped you to answer the question significantly faster? If so, what is it?
  2. What is an "aha" moment you had or a connection you made while working on the problem?
  3. Next time I teach this class, how many points should this problem be worth?
  4. Is there anything you would change about the wording of the problem to help make it clearer? If so, what?
