Choice Exercise 8 [≤10] (TODO - normal subgroups are not transitive)

Prove that the normal subgroup relation \(H \triangleleft G\) is not transitive by doing the following.

  1. On Exam 1, you proved that $A_n \triangleleft S_n$. Repeat this argument here. (Prove the homework 1 hint!)
  2. On HW 5? you proved that conjugation in the symmetric group preserves cycle structure. Show that \(N = \{e, (12)(34), (13)(24), (14)(23)\}\) is a subgroup and is closed under conjugation. (It's closed under conjugation by elements of \(S_n\), so it's definitely closed by conjugation under \(A_n\).) And thus \(N \triangleleft S_4\) and \(H \triangleleft A_4\).
  3. Prove that \(H \cong C_2 \times C_2\) by describing an isomorphism, and briefly say why \(H\) is abelian.
  4. Quickly recall the proof that all subgroups of an abelian group are normal subgroups, and give an example of a proper non-trivial subgroup of \(H \triangleleft N\).
  5. Prove that \(H\) is not a normal subgroup of \(S_4\), and explain why this implies that the normal subgroup relation is not transitive.
