Do the following exercises about quotienting $(\mathbb{R},+)$.
  1. Prove that the set of rotation matrices \[ SO_2(\mathbb{R}) = \left\{\begin{pmatrix}\cos \theta & -\sin \theta \\ \sin \theta & \cos \theta \end{pmatrix}\middle|\, \theta \in \mathbb{R} \right\} \] forms a group under matrix multiplication.
  2. Prove that the map $\phi \colon \mathbb{R} \to SO_2(\mathbb{R})$ given by \[ \phi(x) = \begin{pmatrix}\cos(2\pi x) & -\sin(2\pi x) \\ \sin(2\pi x) & \cos(2\pi x) \end{pmatrix} \] is a homomorphism but not an isomorphism.
  3. Determine the kernel and image of $\phi$.
  4. What group is $\mathbb{R}/\ker(\phi)$ isomorphic to?
