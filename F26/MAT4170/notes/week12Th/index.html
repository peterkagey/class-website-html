Cayley's theorem

We have made it to the last lecture of the term! In these notes, we will prove Cayley's theorem, which appeared briefly in HW 4 Choice Exercise 10, and tells us that every finite group is isomorphic to a subgroup of the symmetric group \(S_n\) for some positive integer \(n\).

We begin by revisiting HW 4 Choice Exercise 10, giving a way to embed \(D_n\), the dihedral group of the \(n\)-gon, into the symmetric group \(S_n\).
