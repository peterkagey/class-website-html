Dihedral group

Today we will be learning about our first family of groups, called the dihedral group of the regular \(n\)-gon, and denoted \(D_n\). These are the symmetries of the equilateral triangle, square, regular pentagon, and so on.

We know that we can rotate the \(n\)-gon by \(2\pi/n\) radians (or \(360^\circ/n\)) and it will look identical to how it looked before. We also know that we can reflect a polygon across a line that goes through it's vertex or the midpoint of its side.
