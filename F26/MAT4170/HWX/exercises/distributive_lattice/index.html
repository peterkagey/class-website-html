A lattice is an algebraic structure \((L, \land, \lor)\) where \(\land,\lor\colon L \times L \to L\) are commutative and associative, and they satisfy the following axioms for all elements \(a, b \in L\): \begin{align*} a \lor (a \land b) &= a \\ a \land (a \lor b) &= a \end{align*}
