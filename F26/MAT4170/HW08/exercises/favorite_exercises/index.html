The next time I teach this class, my students will really like getting to hear from you! Choose a (favorite?) exercise that you did on the homework this term, and write a sentence or two to future students about it.

Post on the #hw-prompts Discord using the following format, but only include the star if you're talking about your favorite exercise from the term. (Obviously, change the exercise and the message.) 

**HW 2, Choice Exercise 6.** ⭐️ This was my favorite exercise of the term because I was really struggling with how to think about cycle notation, and everything clicked with this one.

Here are some examples of what you might say:

  • "Don't make my mistake: I got stuck on this problem because I forgot that not all subgroups are normal!"
  • "Avoid this problem unless it's worth like 10 points, lol"
  • "I'm really glad that I spent time thinking about symmetric groups on HW 2, because they came up again (and again and again)"
  • "This was my favorite problem because it really helped me understand some ideas from linear algebra which had been confusing to me when I first took that class."
