  1. [2] Certain mattress manufacturers recommend flipping a mattress every 6 months to prevent uneven wear, alternating between each of its four symmetries.
    1. Prove that the "mattress flipping" group cannot be generated by a single element, which means that you cannot do the same procedure each six months if you want even wear.
    2. Explain why flipping the mattress over each spring and rotating it $180^\circ$ each fall will hit each of the four symmetries equally.
  2. [2] Explain why the inverse of the permutation $(12345)(6)(789)$ is $(987)(6)(54321)$. And use this principle find the inverse of $\pi = (726314)(95)$ in cycle notation.
  3. [1] Give an example of three permutations $\pi_1, \pi_2, \pi_3 \in S_9$ that commute with $\sigma = (125)(79)$, and two that don't.
  4. [2] There are two elements in $D_4$ that commute with every other element. In other words, there exist two distinct elements $g_1$ and $g_2$ such that $a * g_1 = g_1 * a$ and $a * g_2 = g_2 * a$ for all $a \in D_4$. Find them.
  5. [2] Recall that the order of a group element \(g\) is the least positive number \(k\) such that \(g^k = e\), where \(e\) denotes the identity. What is the greatest possible order of an element in the dihedral group \(D_{12}\)? What is the greatest possible order of an element in the symmetric group \(S_{12}\)?
