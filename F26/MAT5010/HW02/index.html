HW 2 (Week 2–3)

Due on 08 September 2026 at 2:50PM on Gradescope.

This assignment will have you work though this week's lesson on whitespace, environments, and theorem environments. It is designed to take approximately 30 minutes. If you get stuck or are otherwise spending more time than this, reach out to me or a classmate.

Download the following minimal \(\LaTeX\) template, which includes an appendix that shows your source code. Open it in Overleaf or the text editor that you are using, rename the file to HW2.tex, and change the second-to-last line to \lstinputlisting[language=TeX]{HW2.tex} to reflect this change.

When you are done, compile the document (making sure you've updated the \title and \author) and submit it to Gradescope.

Instructions

Write a document that includes examples of everything that we discussed in class (listed below). Don't worry about the content here, just make sure that you demonstrate understanding of each of the concepts.

Use \section{...} and \subsection{...} to organize your submission. At the end of each section write an observation or a question.

Whitespace

  • A paragraph that begins with \noindent
  • A \pagebreak
  • A \vspace{1cm}
  • A \bigskip
  • One line that uses \,, \hspace{1cm}, \quad, and \qquad

Lists

  • An enumerate environment with a few items, nested two levels deep
  • An itemize environment with a few items, nested two levels deep

Tables

  • A tabular environment with a few columns columns, a horizontal line, a vertical line, and at least one center-aligned and one left-aligned column
  • A pmatrix, bmatrix, and matrix with a one sentence description of their differences.

Math environments

  • An align or align* environment with at least two lines
  • An alignat environment with two alignments
  • An equation environment

AMS theorem package

In the preamble, use amsthm to define a numbered theorem-style environment, a definition-style environment, a remark-style environment, and one unnumbered environment: 

\newtheorem{theorem}{Theorem}[section]

\theoremstyle{definition}
\newtheorem{definition}[theorem]{Definition}

\theoremstyle{remark}
\newtheorem{remark}[theorem]{Remark}
\newtheorem*{note*}{Note}

In the body of the document, include:

  1. One theorem, followed by a proof environment
  2. One definition
  3. One remark
  4. One unnumbered note (using note*)
